# Recorded measurements

These files retain the measurements behind the reported results, including
negative comparisons and backend limitations. They are evidence from the stated
checkpoint, software, hardware and input cases, rather than promises for another
machine or a new model version.

Start with the [illustrated report](../docs/technical-report.md) and
[GPU validation guide](../docs/gpu-validation.md). To generate new measurements,
follow the [Mol-JEPA](../docs/tutorials/moljepa.md) or
[Boltz-2](../docs/tutorials/boltz2.md) tutorial. Keep fresh reports under `work/`
and preserve the committed historical measurements.

Tensor snapshots and model weights are local assets and are excluded from Git.
